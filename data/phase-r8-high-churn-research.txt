===================================================================
PHASE R8: HIGH-CHURN / HIGH-EARNING APP RESEARCH (July 2026)
Fresh web research pass — "churn-proof" one-time-need apps
===================================================================

CORE THESIS (validated by this pass):
High churn is NOT a problem if the market replenishes itself. Two
proven patterns are printing money right now:

PATTERN A — Mobile consumer AI utility:
  photo/voice input → instant AI result → hard paywall → weekly sub
  Distribution: TikTok/Instagram UGC creators, not ads or SEO.
  Users churn in weeks; new users arrive daily from short-form video.

PATTERN B — Web one-time-need tool:
  SEO/Google intent → single job done → one-time payment or credits.
  No App Store 30% cut. Churn is the business model (per-event/per-use).

RAW EVIDENCE COLLECTED
===================================================================

[Cal AI — photo calorie tracker]
- Launched May 2024 by two teenagers; $1M sales by Sept 2024
- ~$35M ARR by end 2025, on track for $50M through 2026; ~$1.4M/mo
  gross profit after store fees; acquired by MyFitnessPal Dec 2025
- Sources: techcrunch.com (Mar 2025), cnbc.com (Sep 2025),
  getlatka.com/companies/calai.app, inc.com

[Umax — looksmaxxing face-scan app, Blake Anderson]
- $6M ARR within 3.5 months of launch; $4.2M+ on iOS alone;
  still ~$350-400K/month
- NOTE: heavy ethical criticism (teen mental health) — avoid teen
  looksmaxxing angle; adult skincare/grooming is the cleaner variant

[Quittr — porn-abstinence app, 19-yr-old founder]
- Built in 10 days; $300K/month; $40K in a single day; 1M+ downloads
- "Quit X" category generalizes: vaping, weed, sugar, doomscrolling

["App Mafia" collective (Anderson, Yadegari, Slater, et al.)]
- Combined $80-100M/yr revenue, no investors, 2-3 person teams
- Playbook: viral pain-point + AI wrapper + weekly sub + UGC creators

[Rizz — AI dating keyboard/wingman]
- 7.5M+ downloads; 1M+ MAU in 2025; ~$190K/mo early; $7/week sub

[Scanner/identifier app farms]
- Next Vision Limited (China): 24 identifier apps (CoinSnap, Rock
  Identifier...) ≈ $3M revenue/MONTH combined
- CoinSnap alone: ~$1M/mo iOS + $400K/mo Google Play, ~700K dl/mo
- PictureThis (Glority) long-standing $100M-class plant identifier
- Model: same codebase, new model + keyword niche, weekly sub

[AI photo/headshot]
- PhotoAI (solo founder Pieter Levels): $130K+ MRR, ~87% margin
- Aragon AI: $10M+/yr, 2.3M users. Market maturing/crowded but
  proves one-time-pack pricing works ($35-49 packs)

[Job search / interview prep]
- Resume-builder AI app market: ~$1.4B global 2025 (NA $537.6M)
- Final Round AI: $6.88M seed, 10M+ users, charges $148/mo (!)
- Rezi 4.3M users. AIApply, Seekario, EarnBetter all scaling
- Perfect churn-proof niche: users leave when hired; demand infinite

[Monetization benchmarks — RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps]
- Weekly subscriptions = 55.5% of ALL subscription app revenue
  (up from 43.3% two years prior)
- Weekly + free trial = best 12-month LTV
- Hard paywall converts ~5x freemium (10.7% vs 2.1% D35 dl-to-paid)
- 35% of apps mix subs + consumables/lifetime
- AI apps revenue-per-install $0.63 at day 60 = 2x overall median

[Market macro — Sensor Tower State of Mobile/AI 2026]
- GenAI app IAP revenue tripled in 2025 to $5B+; H1 2026 projected $4B+
- AI app downloads doubled YoY to 3.8B in 2025
- 2025: first year non-game IAP revenue passed games (+21% YoY)
- GenAI time-spent projected to double again H1 2026 (17.2B → 36B hrs)

[Web one-time-need tools]
- BankStatementConverter.com (solo): ~$9K→$16K/mo, SEO-driven,
  credits → subscription
- Wedding photo QR-share market: fragmented (Guestpix, Wedibox,
  GuestCam, WedUploader, Kululu...), per-event pricing $0-150,
  wedding album market ~$3.8B 2025, demand replenishes every season
- Passport/visa photo apps: evergreen compliance niche, 200+ country
  specs, per-photo one-time payment

[Distribution playbook]
- 80% of dev effort should go to onboarding + paywall (expert who ran
  10K+ experiments for Cal AI/Claim; multi-step onboarding → 34% trial
  start rates)
- UGC micro-creators on TikTok targeting a specific demo beats paid UA
- Discovery commerce: awareness → conversion in the same 15 seconds
- TikTok Minis (2026): in-TikTok lightweight apps, 80% shorter
  conversion path — emerging free-organic-traffic channel to watch
